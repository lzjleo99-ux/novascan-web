People · 01

People & Moments

  • —Wedding couples, cake toppers and celebration keepsakes
  • —Family portraits, anniversary gifts, graduation moments
  • —Pets — sleeping dogs and impatient cats welcome

Pets · 02

Pets, exactly as they are.

A pet session takes 20–40 minutes, and most of it is letting them settle in. We capture with infrared — no laser, no contact, no posing. Sleeping dogs, impatient cats, parrots on shoulders: every character is welcome.

  • —A gentle way to plan ahead — the scan takes minutes, the keepsake lasts
  • —Multi-pet sessions — the whole family in one visit
  • —Name engraving, wooden base and gift-box options

Art & product · 03

Objects & Art

From auction-house archives to e-commerce 360° viewers — we capture objects with the surface fidelity that photos can't carry.

Typical jobs

  • —Sculpture & artwork archives for insurers, estates and galleries
  • —Exact replicas — enlarged, reduced or re-cast from the scan
  • —Product prototypes digitised for iteration and CAD comparison

Deliverables

  • —Watertight mesh for 3D printing (STL / 3MF)
  • —Web-ready model with shareable 3D viewer
  • —Measured archive copy (PLY) with provenance notes

Large objects · 04

Vehicles & Machinery

Handheld wireless capture means no disassembly, no crane, no studio — we scan where the machine stands, in one session.

Full bodies

Cars, motorcycles, trucks, trailers and agri/industrial equipment — complete external geometry for design, wraps, body kits and documentation.

Reverse parts

Out-of-production components re-modelled from the scan — brackets, panels, fittings — delivered as print-ready or CAD-ready files.

Documentation

Accident and condition documentation with measurable 3D records — objective evidence for insurers and courts.

Micro · 05

Small Parts & Jewellery

High-definition parallel-laser mode captures fine geometry with point spacing down to 0.05 mm — the difference between "looks right" and measures right.

Typical jobs

  • —Gears, seals and precision components for manufacturing
  • —Jewellery masters — rings, pendants, settings — for casting and resizing
  • —Enclosures, brackets and miniatures for product development

Precision stack

  • —0.05 mm point distance in HD mode
  • —Marker or photogrammetry alignment for measured accuracy
  • —Datum-based measurement and deviation reports on request

Freeform · 06

Complex Freeform

Turbine blades, castings, organic surfaces, ergonomic shapes — geometry that defeats calipers, CMM probes and flatbed scanners alike.

Engineering

First-article and deviation inspection against CAD, aligned on datums you define (3-2-1, RPS or best-fit).

Art & design

Enlarge, reduce or mirror sculptures and models — proportionally faithful to a fraction of a millimetre.

Manufacturing

Casting patterns, moulds and masters derived directly from scanned surfaces.

Heritage · 07

Heritage & Museum

Non-contact, zero-risk digitisation for artefacts that must not be touched — conservation records today, replicas and interactive exhibits tomorrow.

For institutions

  • —Condition and damage documentation with measurable archives
  • —Exhibition replicas and touchable copies for visitors
  • —Interactive 3D web exhibits and virtual reunification of collections

How we work

  • —Scanning on-site in the gallery — objects never leave the building
  • —Laser or IR modes chosen per object; no contact at any point
  • —Data ownership stays with the institution — full NDA on request
