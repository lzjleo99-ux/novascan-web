Questions

Asked often, answered straight.

How accurate is "accurate"?
Point distance down to 0.05 mm and volumetric accuracy of 0.04 + 0.06 mm/m — and we publish the measured numbers with every job.

Do you come to us?
Yes — fully wireless scanning means we bring the studio to your factory floor, showroom or living room, anywhere in Serbia and the region.

Your choice of mesh formats (STL / OBJ / PLY / 3MF), measurement reports, and a shareable 3D web viewer for every project.
