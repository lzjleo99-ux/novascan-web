How we reach the numbers

Three disciplines behind one scan.

Mode discipline

Crossed lines for coverage, parallel lines for detail, infrared for people. The mode is chosen per object — never one-size-fits-all.

Alignment strategy

Markers, features, texture or photogrammetric scale bars — the alignment scheme is planned before we scan, so global accuracy holds across the whole object.

Verified surfaces

Live quality maps flag low-confidence areas; we rescan them on the spot. Meshes leave the studio watertight, cleaned and inspected.

Processing pipeline

From raw cloud to deliverable.

01

Clean

Noise filtering, small-floating-part removal, spike elimination.

02

Mesh

Watertight or semi-watertight meshing, auto & manual hole filling, curvature-based refinement.

03

Measure

Datum creation (points/lines/planes), 3-2-1 alignment, distances, areas and volumes.

04

Package

Format conversion, simplification to spec, report generation and web-viewer publishing.

FAQ

Everything clients ask before booking.

Is scanning safe for people and animals?
Yes. People and pets are captured in infrared mode — no laser projection at all. For objects, laser capture is still non-contact and eye-safe at working distance.
Can you scan shiny, black or transparent surfaces?
Reflective and dark surfaces have a dedicated scanning mode. Transparent surfaces may need a thin washable coating — we always test a small area first and tell you before booking.
Do you sign NDAs?
Always available, routinely signed. Your scans, models and photos are never published or used as references without written consent.
How fast is delivery?
Typical single-object jobs deliver within 48 hours of scanning; larger on-site campaigns within five working days. Deadlines are fixed in the quote.
Can you produce a CAD model from a scan?
Yes — from a clean mesh we prepare STEP-ready surfaces or parametric re-modelling for reverse engineering, quoted per object complexity.
What does it cost?
Pricing depends on size, complexity and deliverable. Send a photo and the goal — you get a fixed quote within one business day. No hourly surprises.
